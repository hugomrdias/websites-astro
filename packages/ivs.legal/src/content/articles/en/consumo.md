## Tell the story

A purchase can lead to a series of conversations and documents. This sample text shows how to bring that story together clearly, without anticipating a solution for a specific case.

## Keep the sequence

- Identify the purchase and available documents.
- Put previous conversations in order.
- Record what you would like to clarify.

## Prepare a summary

A short summary can include what you were looking for, what you received and the conversations that followed. Mark any missing information so it can be clarified during the meeting.
