## Describe the project

Before getting into the details, it can help to describe the project on one page: what you want to do, with whom and when. This sample text shows how an article can help make a conversation more concrete.

## Map the decisions

- Who is involved in the project?
- Which resources are already available?
- Which decisions are still open?

## Record expectations

People who share an idea do not always imagine the same path. Writing down each person’s expectations helps identify topics that deserve their own conversation, from organising the work to setting a project timeline.

## Prepare the conversation

A future article can explore these topics through examples reviewed by the team. A project description and a list of questions can be a starting point for a first meeting.
