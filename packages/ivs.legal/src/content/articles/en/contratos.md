## Start with the questions

A document can bring many decisions together in a single signature. This sample article starts with a simple idea: make time to read, note your questions and organise the context for a conversation.

## Bring the context together

- What is the purpose of the agreement?
- Which documents and messages belong to the conversation?
- Which points need a clearer explanation?

## From the document to the conversation

A short list of questions can give a meeting structure. Separate what you already understand, what you want to confirm and what still needs discussion. Keep documents in date order so the sequence is easier to follow.
