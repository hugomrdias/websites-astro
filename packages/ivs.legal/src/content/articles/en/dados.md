## Understand the journeys

Information moves across teams, tools and tasks. In this sample article, the starting point is to observe those journeys and find a simple way to describe them.

## Draw a map

- What information enters the organisation?
- Which teams use it day to day?
- Which tools form part of its journey?

## Name the questions

Describing a process often reveals questions without clear answers. Recording those questions helps prepare a conversation with the people who understand each stage.
