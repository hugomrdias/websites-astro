## Organise the information

Moving home brings together conversations, visits and documents from different sources. This editorial example explores how to organise that information, without setting out rules or recommendations for a particular transaction.

## Create a property folder

- Gather the documents received.
- Keep relevant messages in date order.
- Note questions that remain unanswered.

## Build a timeline

One page with the main events can help explain the situation. Separate confirmed dates from proposed dates and identify who sent each document. The aim is to make the material easier to consult.

## Bring your questions

This topic can later be developed with reviewed legal content. Every property and every transaction has its own context, which needs to be understood before drawing conclusions.
