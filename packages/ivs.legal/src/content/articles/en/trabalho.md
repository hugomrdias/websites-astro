## Start with the context

Workplace questions can involve several moments and people. This demonstration text shows a possible editorial structure for presenting a situation clearly.

## Gather the material

- Identify the main events.
- Organise the available documents.
- Write down questions for the meeting.

## Separate facts and questions

A short account can distinguish what happened from what still needs confirmation. Avoid filling gaps in memory: identifying uncertainty is also useful information for a conversation.

## Make time to explain

A future article can explore workplace topics with reviewed information. Considering any specific question depends on understanding the circumstances and the relevant documents.
